# Mini-Games

Press **PLAY** on the home screen to see the games. Your cat has to be awake and not out hunting. If your cat's energy is below 15, PLAY skips the menu and takes it straight to a nap.

Games tire your cat out. A good result cheers it up, and a win makes it a little less grumpy.

## Play menu

| Game | Hairballs | Energy cost |
|---|---|---|
| Chase the Laser | Up to 3 bonus hairballs in the round | 14 |
| Scratching Post | 1 per star | 17 |
| Maze Run | Up to 2 pickups in the maze | 17 |
| Knock It Off | Up to 2 pickups on the table | 17 |
| Magic Cat | 1 to 3 (needs a box) | none |

Every game except Magic Cat pays Fun for the stars you earn: about 17 for one star, 25 for two and 33 for three. A game with no stars pays no Fun.

## Chase the Laser

Your finger is the laser dot. Keep the dot away from your cat as it chases, and the cat pounces at the dot whenever it can. The round lasts 20 seconds. The cat gets faster and more eager as the round goes on, and it cannot pounce in the first 3 seconds.

- **Stars:** 0 catches = 3 stars, 1 catch = 2 stars, 2 catches = 1 star, 3 or more catches = no stars.
- **Bonus hairballs:** one lies in the middle of the floor at the start. Two more pop up with 8 and 3 seconds left. Drag your finger over one to collect it.

## Scratching Post

Arrows scroll in towards a target ring. When an arrow reaches the ring, swipe in the direction it points (up or down) anywhere below the top bar. Timing is what counts.

- **Stars:** 3 stars for hitting every arrow, 2 stars for 80% or more, and 1 star for 60% or more.
- **Hairballs:** one per star.

## Maze Run

Your cat follows your finger through the maze. You have 30 seconds to collect loot and carry it home to the little house in the maze. Heavier loot slows your cat a little.

- Each maze has one clock pickup that adds 10 seconds.
- Each maze has two hairball pickups that give 1 hairball each (three for a Sphynx, who never coughs up hairballs at home).
- Hauling loot home counts towards your stars: 9 or more loot is 1 star, 14 or more is 2 stars, and 23 or more is 3 stars.
- If you haul everything home early, the run ends straight away.

## Knock It Off

Your cat strolls along the table towards the things it is about to shove off. Tap the item it is lined up to shove, and it stays put. The round lasts 20 seconds, and if five things get knocked off the game is over.

Two hairballs roll onto the table with 16 and 8 seconds left. Tap one before it rolls away to collect it.

- **Stars:** save more items and knock off fewer for more stars.

## Magic Cat

You need a box in your house to play. The game puts a box over a spot on the floor. Is the cat under it? Watch for a wobble, which means someone is inside.

- The game has five rounds.
- **Hairballs:** 3 right answers = 1 hairball, 4 right = 2, and 5 right = 3.

## Feeding (pour)

Pressing **FEED** starts the pouring game. Tilt the device to pour kibble for 5 seconds. A good pour fills your cat and keeps its weight steady. Overfilling or spilling makes it heavier. There are no hairballs in this game. See [[Care and Needs]].

## Lap Cat (nap)

Lap Cat is the nap game. Hold the device still, like a cat on your lap, and your cat's energy fills up. A few bumps will wake it. Once it is fully rested, the nap is over.

## Litter box

The litter box is a game too. Dig out the poops with your finger, then carry each one to the can and tilt to drop it in. Sometimes a lost hairball is buried in the litter, and you can dig it up for 1 hairball (up to six a day). See [[Care and Needs]].

## Explore

The biggest adventure is Explore, which unlocks when your cat is an adult. See [[Explore]].
