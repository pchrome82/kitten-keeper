# Shop

Hairballs are the money in Kitten Keeper. Press **SHOP** on the home screen to open the shop menu:

**WARDROBE**, **GOODS**, **FURNITURE**, **BOXES** and **HOMES**.

Each page has a **BACK** button that returns you to the menu. If you open the Wardrobe from the shop, BACK takes you to the shop. A price button turns grey if you cannot afford the item. Tapping a grey button does nothing, and the tap never passes on to the button next to it.

## Goods

| Item | Price | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Catnip Treat** | 3 | Gives 40 Energy and 10 Fun. Your cat goes a bit wild and runs around. You cannot use it while your cat sleeps, or when its Energy is already above 80. |
| **Vet Visit** | 10 | Gives 45 Health and makes your cat a little less grumpy. |
| **Auto Litter-Bot** | 120 | Removes one poop from the litter box about every minute, so you do not have to scoop by hand. It shows OWNED once bought. |

## Furniture

Furniture helps your cat nap better and keeps its Fun up for longer. The shop shows two numbers. **Nap** is how much better your cat rests in that spot. **Fun** is how much more slowly its Fun drains.

| Item | Price | Nap | Fun |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Floor Cat Bed** | 5 | +7% | |
| **Ripped Chair** | 10 | +15% | +5% |
| **Cat Tree** | 15 | +15% | +15% |
| **Luxury Chair** | 25 | +30% | +10% |
| **Cat Tree + Bed** | 45 | +50% | +20% |

Some notes:

- Only the best nap spot you own counts for napping. For Fun, the chairs and toys add up, but only the bigger of Cat Tree and Cat Tree + Bed counts.
- Every furniture purchase comes with a free **Cardboard Box**, if you have no box, a Tissue Box or a Shoebox.
- Toys are not sold any more. You find them while exploring: a Toy Mouse, a Yarn Ball, a Feather Wand and a Crinkle Tunnel. Each one slows your cat's Fun drain a little, and the Feather Wand helps the most. With all four toys, a kitten's Fullness and Fun last much longer overnight.

## Boxes

Cats love boxes. A box is a short-lived toy, and any cat can use any box. Buying a new box replaces the one you have.

| Box | Price | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| **Tissue Box** | 2 | 30 minutes |
| **Shoebox** | 8 | 2 hours |
| **Cardboard Box** | 14 | 6 hours |
| **Sturdy Crate** | 50 | Forever |

When a box runs out, it falls apart (or, for a tissue box, your cat shreds it). The BOXES page shows how long your current box has left. Boxes cannot be sold back. A box also gives a small nap bonus, and you need one to play Magic Cat. See [[Mini-Games]].

## Homes

Buy a home to move your cat into a new place. Your cat's things come along with it.

| Home | Price | View |
|---|---|---|
| **Grimy Cartoon** | Free (your starting home) | City view, cheap rent |
| **Cozy Treehouse** | 15 | Forest view, all wood |
| **Groovy '70s** | 25 | Shag carpet, city view |
| **Modern Apartment** | 35 | Floor-to-ceiling glass |
| **Seaside Villa** | 45 | Sea view |
| **Classy Log Cabin** | 55 | River and forest, with a fireplace. You also get this for beating the Alley King for the first time. See [[Explore]]. |
| **A secret home** | 100 | One more home is a secret. Keep an eye out for it. |

Once you own a home, press **MOVE IN** to switch to it. Moving is free.

## Hairball balance

Your hairball balance shows at the top of the shop. If your cat has a vet tab, the balance shows in red as a negative number. Hairballs you earn pay off the tab first. See [[Care and Needs]].
