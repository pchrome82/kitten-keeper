# Care and Needs

Your cat has a few needs that you look after every day. Keep them up and your cat stays happy, healthy and lives its full month. Let them slide and it gets grumpy, sick, or runs away.

## The meters

| Meter | Where to see it | What it means | How to look after it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fullness** | Top bar (fish) and Stats | How fed your cat is. Empty means hungry. | Feed it with FEED. |
| **Fun** | Top bar (ball) and Stats | How entertained it is. | Play games, pet it, and use toys and furniture. |
| **Energy** | Top bar (moon) and Stats | How rested it is. Low energy means a nap. | Let it sleep, nap in a bed or box, press CAT NAP in the PLAY menu, or have a catnip treat from the shop. |
| **Health** | Top bar (heart) and Stats | How well your cat is. At 0 it goes to the vet. | Keep it fed, clean and not too heavy. Vet visits help. |
| **Weight** | Stats | How heavy it is. Ideal is 35 as a kitten and 50 as an adult. | Feed carefully and play. See the Weight section below. |
| **Grumpy** | Stats | How fed up it is with you. At 100 it runs away. | Keep the meters up, clean the mess, and pet it. |

Fullness and Fun fall on the clock, whether you are looking or not. Energy only rises while your cat sleeps.

A top-bar meter turns red when it drops below 25.

## Mood

The Stats screen shows your cat's mood:

| Mood | When you see it |
|---|---|
| **Happy** | Fun over 70 and Fullness over 45, with nothing else wrong. |
| **Content** | Things are fine. This is the normal mood. |
| **Unhappy** | Fullness or Fun below 25, Grumpy at 55 or more, three or more poops in the box, or mess on the floor. |
| **GRUMPY** | Grumpy at 80 or more, or just woken up roughly. |
| **Sleeping** | Your cat is asleep. |

## Day and night

- **Day (8 am to 8 pm):** your cat is up and about, whether you are there or not.
- **Night (8 pm to 8 am):** your cat naps on the screen. Fullness and Fun creep down slowly.
- **Waking:** a touch wakes a napping cat for a feed or a game. Fifteen minutes after your last touch, it goes back to sleep.
- **Shake or lift:** shaking or lifting the device while your cat sleeps wakes it up grumpy.

On a normal day, Fullness and Fun each run from full to empty in about 12 hours. A kitten needs you more often: its meters last about 2 hours.

A plan that works for most cats: about three meals and four games a day.

## Growing up

Your cat grows through four stages. Age moves faster when it is well looked after, and slower when it is not.

| Stage | Age | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Kitten** | First half a day | Needs you most often. Does not hunt. Meals digest quickly. |
| **Junior** | Half a day to 1 day | Can hunt and go on Explore once it is an adult. |
| **Adult** | 1 to 26 days | Can go on Explore. Its ideal weight is 50. |
| **Senior** | 26 to 31 days | Slower in games and a little weaker in Explore fights. |

A well-kept cat lives about a month. At 31 days it retires. A cat that is unwell, overweight, grumpy or neglected ages faster and may not reach 31 days.

## Weight

Your cat's weight matters. Heavier cats are slower in games and in Explore, and their Health falls if they are very overweight.

- **Ideal weight:** 35 as a kitten, rising to 50 as an adult.
- **Too heavy:** every bit over ideal makes it a little slower, up to about a third slower when it is 35 over. Being 30 or more over ideal slowly costs Health, and 45 or more over makes Health fall faster.
- **Too skinny:** more than 13 under ideal. Pour in the green part of the feeding meter to put weight back on.
- **Burning weight:** play and Explore burn weight off, so a cat that is a little heavy can slim down through games.

## Feeding

Press **FEED** and tilt the device to pour kibble into the bowl. Pour steadily into the green part of the meter. Too much or a spill makes the cat heavier, and too little leaves it skinny. A completely stuffed cat refuses food.

Only kibble is sold and poured now.

## The litter box (POO)

Each poop lands in the litter box. Press **POO** and:

1. Run your finger through the sand to dig up the poops.
2. Drag each poop to the can.
3. Tilt the device to drop it in.

If you leave poops in the box, your cat gets grumpier, and it meows when there are three or more. A cat that is very grumpy may poop on the floor instead. Tap the mess on the home screen to clean it up.

The **Auto Litter-Bot** (from the [[Shop]]) removes one poop about every minute, so you do not have to scoop by hand.

## Hairballs

Your cat coughs up hairballs now and then while you are around. Each one lands on the floor. Tap a hairball to collect it for one hairball. You can have up to three on the floor. A hairball left alone for half an hour turns into mess on the floor.

How often a cat coughs up hairballs depends on its breed. Sphynx cats never do, but a good petting session earns them one now and then. See [[Breeds]].

## Hunting and catches

Adult cats and juniors sometimes go hunting when they are happy and healthy (Fun 30 or more, Fullness 25 or more, Health 30 or more). They come back with a catch that waits on the floor. Tap it to collect hairballs:

| Catch | Hairballs |
|---|---|
| Mouse | 2 |
| Bird | 3 |
| Lizard | 3 |
| Fish | 5 |
| Golden Mouse | 12 |

Catches left on the floor for 15 minutes while you are there are lost. A cat may also leave a catch for you while you are away, during the day.

Sometimes a mouse dashes across the home room. Tap it while it runs to catch it for one hairball.

## Meows

Your cat meows when it is hungry (Fullness below 30), when there is litter to clean, or when it is bored (Fun below 20). It only meows while you are around.

## Petting

Tap the cat to pet it, and hold to purr. Petting raises Fun and lowers Grumpy. Cuddly cats love it even more.

## Health and the vet

Health falls when your cat is starved, has mess lying around for too long, has four or more poops in the box, or is very overweight. It rises when your cat is fed, clean, content and not too heavy.

If Health reaches 0, your cat is rushed to the vet:

- The visit costs 10 hairballs. If you do not have enough, you still pay what you have, and the rest goes on a tab. Hairballs you find later pay off the tab first, and the tab shows in red.
- Your cat comes back with 45 Health.
- If it reaches 0 Health a third time, it runs away instead.

You can also buy a Vet Visit from the [[Shop]] for 10 hairballs. It gives 45 Health.

## Grumpy and running away

Grumpy goes up when your cat is neglected: hungry, bored, dirty, or in poor health. It falls with good care, play and petting. The game warns you when Grumpy reaches 60 and again at 85.

At 100 Grumpy, your cat runs away and the game ends. Look after it.

## How a cat's life ends

| Ending | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Retired** | At 31 days, your cat retires to Grandma's sunny garden. You get 10 hairballs plus 2 for each catch it made. Then you can name and adopt a new kitten. |
| **Ran away** | Your cat leaves because of neglect. Your hairballs go back to 0, your Auto Litter-Bot is removed, and the vet tab is wiped. Your furniture, outfits and homes stay with you. Then you can name and adopt a new kitten. |

Each cat is kept in your journal, with its photos, when it leaves.

## Special days

| Day | When | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Birthday** | Once a year, on the birthday picked for your cat | 5 hairballs, 20 Fun, a party, and a gift box |
| **Weekly birthday** | Every week of your cat's age | 5 hairballs, 20 Fun, and a party hat for the day |
| **Halloween** | From 24 October | A Halloween greeting, a gift box, and a pumpkin hat when your cat has no hat on |
| **Holidays** | 20 to 26 December | A greeting, a gift box, and a Santa hat when your cat has no hat on |
| **New Year** | 31 December and 1 January | A greeting, a gift box, and a party hat when your cat has no hat on |
| **Winter** | December to February | Snow in the window now and then |

A birthday or holiday gift box is a wrapped box on the floor at home. Tap it to open it. See [[Wardrobe]] for what is inside.

### Good-care streak

At your first visit home each day, a cat that is healthy (Health 70 or more), not grumpy (Grumpy under 40) and fed (Fullness 25 or more) adds a day to a streak. Five good days in a row earn a gift box. Any day that is not good starts the streak over.

## Journal and album

The **JOURNAL** in the menu keeps your milestones, and a photo of your cat at each life stage. Milestones are shared by every cat you raise:

- First meal
- First hairball
- A catch from a hunt
- 3 stars in a game
- First Explore trip
- Beat a wild cat
- Beat the Alley King
- Found the hidden cave
- Explored together
- First birthday
- A full lap nap
- Their first furniture
- All grown up
- A wise old senior

The album keeps up to 24 past cats. The Hall of Cats in the journal shows the cats you have raised.
