# Kitten Keeper

Kitten Keeper is a Tamagotchi-style virtual pet cat for the Waveshare ESP32-S3 touch boards. Feed your cat, play with it, keep its litter box clean, dress it up, and watch it grow up over about a month of real time.

Your cat lives on the screen. You tap, tilt and drag to look after it. Hairballs are the money: earn them, spend them in the shop, and use them to dress your cat in outfits, buy furniture and move into new homes.

## Supported boards

| Board | Firmware file |
|---|---|
| Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.8 | `kitten-keeper-<version>-amoled18.bin` |
| Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16 | `kitten-keeper-<version>-amoled216.bin` |

It has only been tested on Waveshare boards. It is an experimental hobby project, shared as is, with no warranty.
