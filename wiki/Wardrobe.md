# Wardrobe

Dress your cat up in hats, glasses, neckwear and body costumes. Open the Wardrobe from the shop.

## How it works

- Pick one of the four tabs: **HAT**, **EYES**, **NECK** or **BODY**. Each cat can wear one item in each tab.
- Tap an item to select it. Its name and price appear below your cat, and the button under them acts on it:
  - **BUY** buys it with hairballs, and your cat tries it on straight away.
  - **WEAR** puts on an item you already own.
  - **TAKE OFF** removes the item in that tab. Select the first, plain cell ("none") to show it.
- Items you have not got are dimmed in the list.
- Wearing three or more items shows **Dressed to impress!**

## Gift boxes

A wrapped gift box turns up on the floor at home:

- on your cat's birthday,
- at Halloween, the holidays and New Year, and
- after five well-cared-for days in a row.

Tap it to open it. It gives you one of the five gift-only costumes you do not have yet (Star Shades, Monocle, 3-D Specs, Pearls or Necktie), and your cat tries it on straight away. Once you own all five, a gift box gives 10 hairballs instead. See [[Care and Needs]].

## Seasonal hats

These hats are not for sale. When your cat has no hat on, it wears a seasonal one on the right day:

- a **Party Hat** on its birthday, its weekly birthday, and New Year,
- a **Santa Hat** over the holidays (20 to 26 December), and
- a **Pumpkin hat** at Halloween (from 24 October).

## Rewards from Explore

The Explore costumes are rewards for helping the neighbours, finding the Hermit's secret and beating the Alley King. See [[Explore]] for how to earn each one.
