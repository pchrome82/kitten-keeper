# Breeds

Every cat is one of seven breeds, which you choose when you adopt. Each breed has its own look and its own stats. A stat of 100 is average.

## The seven breeds

| Breed | Speed | Strength | Weight gain | Hairballs | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tabby** | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 | Classic. Loud. Orange. |
| **Siamese** | 120 | 90 | 95 | 80 | Fast and chatty. |
| **Tuxedo** | 90 | 100 | 100 | 110 | Dressed for dinner. |
| **Void Cat** | 110 | 90 | 100 | 100 | Stealthy. Stares. |
| **Calico** | 100 | 100 | 100 | 110 | Patches of attitude. |
| **Sphynx** | 130 | 100 | 80 | 0 | Hairless, light and quick. Never coughs up hairballs. Burns weight off faster in games. |
| **Skogkatt** | 80 | 110 | 90 | 120 | Big floof. Hits hard. |

What each stat does:

- **Speed:** how quick the cat is in the games and on Explore. In an Explore fight, a fast cat sometimes lands a free DOUBLE SWIPE and dodges more of the wild cat's swipes.
- **Strength:** how much damage each hit does in an Explore fight. Weight adds a little: up to +20% when heavy, down to -10% when skinny. A cat with 1.2 or more Strength lands a BODY SLAM for half as much again.
- **Weight gain:** how quickly the cat puts on weight from food. A low number means it stays slimmer.
- **Hairballs:** how often it coughs up hairballs. A low number means fewer. The Sphynx never does.

The adoption screen shows Hair, Speed and Strength as bars. The Stats screen shows the cat's current Speed and Strength, which change with its weight, health, age and charms.

## Adopting

All seven breeds are free to adopt. Pick one on the adoption screen and tap **ADOPT!**. See [[Getting Started]].

## Changing breed

You can change your cat's breed mid-game from **MENU → CHANGE BREED**. Each switch costs 10 hairballs, even if you switch back to a breed you have had before. Your cat keeps its age, name and needs.

## Personality

Every cat gets a random personality when it moves in. It nudges the cat's needs and habits a little:

| Personality | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Lazy** | Naps a lot. Tires and gets bored more slowly. Moves a little more slowly in games. |
| **Curious** | Loves the window. Gets bored faster. |
| **Playful** | Games are extra fun (a quarter more Fun from each game). Tires a little faster. |
| **Foodie** | Always hungry for more. Its Fullness drops faster. |
| **Cuddly** | Petting means the world. Gets more Fun from petting, and minds being ignored more. |
| **Brave** | Hits a bit harder in fights. |

The Stats screen shows your cat's personality.
