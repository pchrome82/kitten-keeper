# Explore

Explore is the big outdoor adventure. Once your cat is an adult, take it on a trip across a little map of gardens, a river, caves, a downtown and some secrets. Visit neighbours, open chests, fish, catch critters and fight wild cats.

## Before you set off

- **Adult only.** Explore unlocks when your cat grows up. See [[Care and Needs]].
- **Energy.** You need 50 Energy to set off. Each trip costs 50 Energy when you get home. If your cat has less than 50 Energy, the Explore button is not greyed out: tapping it shows a pop-up explaining that Explore needs 50 Energy, with a CAT NAP button to nap first.
- **Each new screen costs energy.** Walking onto a new screen costs 10 Energy. The Trail Paws (from the first wild cat you beat with a hairball) bring that down to 8, and the Cave Crystal takes off another 4 (see the table below).

| Items you have | Energy per new screen |
|---|---|
| Neither | 10 |
| Trail Paws | 8 |
| Cave Crystal | 6 |
| Both | 4 |

- **Trips can be resumed.** If you leave a trip, you can pick it up again while your cat is awake.
- **Ending a trip.** A trip ends when your cat walks through the front door of its home. It also ends if your cat gets too tired (Energy below 8), or if it loses a fight. Losing a fight costs 6 Fun and makes your cat a little grumpier.
- **Fun from a trip.** Your cat earns Fun from what it did: critters caught, fights won, new places found and screens visited.

## Your map

Every player's world is different. The neighbours, the caves, the garden and the nook land on different screens for each player, and the woods grow differently too. These parts stay the same for everyone:

- **Home** is the bottom-left screen.
- **The river** runs down the middle of the map. Fish swim along it, and there are bridges.
- **Downtown** is the rightmost column of the map.
- **The Alley King's lair** is the top screen, just left of downtown, behind a moat.

Your home, the river, downtown, the moat and every inside (houses and caves) are always the same.

## Moving around

- **Tap a spot** and your cat walks there by itself. A quick tap while it is walking stops it.
- **Hold and drag** to steer.
- **Walk off an edge** to slide onto the next screen.
- **Tap things next to your cat** to use them: signs, nests, loose rocks, bushes, trash, chests and neighbours.
- **Downtown has traffic.** Cars drive along the road one at a time. If one catches your cat, you hear a SCREECH: your cat loses 8 Energy and becomes a little grumpier, and it loses a hairball too if it has one. Your cat then jumps back onto the sidewalk. Look both ways before you cross.

## Hairballs lying around

Some things hide hairballs. Rocks, nests and trash can each be searched once a day, and the total from these finds is capped at 20 a day.

| Where | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Loose rock** (one per screen) | 60% chance of 1 hairball |
| **Nest** in a tree (one per screen) | 50% chance of 1 hairball |
| **Trash** downtown (each half of a screen) | 70% chance of 1 hairball |
| **Tall grass** | Now and then, a hairball is hiding in it |

## Fishing

Stand on the bank of the river, next to a fish, and tap it. Your cat catches it, and its Fullness goes up. You can catch up to five fish on one trip.

## Critters

Creep up on critters to catch them. Some only come out at certain times or weather:

- Mice, birds and butterflies appear on most screens. Fish swim in the river.
- Frogs come out in the rain, by the river.
- Fireflies come out at night.
- Pigeons are in downtown.

The Journal shows which critters and places you have found.

## Neighbours

Walk up to a neighbour's house and go in to talk to them. Each neighbour has a little request.

| Neighbour | Their request | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Grandma Tabby** (the kitchen house) | Bring her 3 fish from the river. | Lace Collar and 3 hairballs |
| **Old Tom** (the cluttered den) | Catch 3 mice for him. | Wizard Hat and 3 hairballs |
| **Miss Whiskers** (the house with the flowery garden) | Find her pink yarn ball, which rolled into a garden. | Flower Crown, 3 hairballs and a Yarn Ball toy |

Some neighbours' houses have a bed in them. One house with a bed is always close to home (within 2 screens), so a tired cat can always find a nap. On some days a stray cat curls up on one of these beds. Beat it to use the bed. Stepping on a bed gives your cat a nap that refills Energy by 40% (once per bed each trip).

## Chests

Chests hold treasure. Each one can only be opened once.

| Chest | Where | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Left chest** | The Old Cave | A brass key, which opens the Alley King's gate |
| **Right chest** | The Old Cave | 3 hairballs and a Toy Mouse |
| **Chest in the garden** | The Fenced Garden. Only a slim cat (near its ideal weight) can squeeze through the gap. | 2 hairballs and a pink Yarn Ball |
| **Chest in the nook** | The Boulder Nook. Only a chonky cat (well over its ideal weight) can shove the boulder out of the way. | 4 hairballs and a Feather Wand |

Some outfits are rewards too. See [[Wardrobe]].

## Secrets

Some secrets are hidden away. Tap, look and explore to find them.

- **Hidden cave.** A thick bush on one screen hides a cave. Tap the bush to find the Hidden Hermit. The Hidden Hermit sells a Wild Amulet for 40 hairballs, which gives +15% attack and +15% speed.
- **Cave Goggles.** Tap the cracked lump on the back wall of the Hermit's Cave, behind the hermit. You get the Cave Goggles (an Eyes costume) for free, once.
- **Cave Crystal.** Look for a crack in the floor of the Old Cave. Step into it to drop into the dark Deep Tunnels, where a chest holds a glowing Cave Crystal and a Crinkle Tunnel toy. The Cave Crystal lowers the Energy cost of each screen by 4.

## The Hermit

The Hermit lives in the Hermit's Cave. He sells a Cozy Charm for 10 hairballs, which makes your cat's needs drop 15% slower.

## Fights

Fights happen when you walk into a wild cat, or when a stray is curled up on a bed. Starting any fight costs 3 Energy.

### Wild cats

A wild cat has 3 hits to take and your cat has 3 hearts. Press **SCRATCH** when the marker is in the green zone. Each hit takes one of the wild cat's hits. If your cat is very chonky (20 or more over its ideal weight), a hit counts double.

You can also throw a hairball. It costs one hairball, and it knocks the wild cat out if it lands. Throws almost always land.

If you miss, the wild cat may swipe back and cost you a heart.

- **Win:** you get 1 hairball and lose 8 Energy.
- **First win with a hairball:** you get the Trail Paws, and each new screen costs 8 Energy instead of 10. See the table above.

### The Alley King

The Alley King is the big boss. Find the key in the Old Cave's left chest, then walk up to his gate and it opens.

The King takes 10 hairball hits to beat. Scratching does nothing to him, so throw hairballs. He dodges about a third of them, and each one costs you a hairball.

You have 4 hearts. The King crouches before he leaps. When he leaps, the **DEFEND** button turns red. Tap it while he is in the air to block the leap. A leap you do not defend costs you a heart. Some crouches are fake-outs, so wait for the real one. If you lose all your hearts, your trip ends.

- **First win:** you get the Crown and the Classy Log Cabin home, and it costs 15 Energy.
- **Rematch:** after your first win, you can challenge him again. Each rematch costs 3 Energy to start and 15 Energy to win, and pays out one prize:

| Prize | How you get it |
|---|---|
| Cowboy Hat | A random rematch prize, until you have it |
| Bandana | A random rematch prize, until you have it |
| Angel Wings | A random rematch prize, until you have it |
| 20 hairballs | Once you have all three of the above |

## Explore Together

To start a shared trip, press **PLAY**, then **EXPLORE**, then **EXPLORE TOGETHER** in the small pop-up. (**SOLO EXPLORE** starts a normal trip instead, and **BACK** closes the pop-up.) Together needs a second Kitten Keeper device nearby.

Up to four devices can explore together over a direct radio link. No router needed. The match uses the classic map, the same for everyone, and no one can go inside a house during a match. With less than 50 Energy, EXPLORE shows the "Too tired to explore" pop-up with a CAT NAP button first, as it does for a solo trip.

- **Squares.** Players race to claim squares of the map. The player with the most squares gets 3 hairballs. Anyone with at least one square gets 1 hairball.
- **King Raid.** Everyone fights the Alley King together, and the team's hits all count. Win the raid and each player gets 3 hairballs. Anyone who landed a hit gets 1 hairball. Hairballs you throw in a raid do not come out of your own balance.
